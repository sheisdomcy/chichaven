About Chic Haven

More than a shop - your style partner in Enugu.

Who We Are

Chic Haven is a fashion store in Enugu that sells quality, affordable clothes, shoes and accessories for men and women. We believe everyone deserves to look good without spending too much.

Our History

Chic Haven started in 2022 with one small shop in Enugu. We started with a few dresses and shirts. Today, we serve hundreds of customers who trust us for trendy and durable fashion.

What We Do

  • Sell trendy dresses, shirts, jeans and native wears
  • Sell shoes, bags, watches and accessories
  • Help customers choose outfits for work, church, parties and weddings

Our Mission

To provide stylish, affordable fashion for everyone.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted fashion brand in Enugu and beyond.

Our Objectives

Quality clothes, fair prices and excellent customer service.
